# Phase 2 — Thesis-Break Criteria

**When to read this file:** Only when a position is actually under pressure — a bad earnings report, a sharp drawdown, a news event, or an urge to sell. Routine weekly check-ins do NOT need this file. Load it on demand to run a thesis re-check.

**The rule:** Thesis-break criteria are the *only* legitimate reason to reconsider a position before the 6-month review (Nov 11, 2026). Volatility ≠ thesis break. A bad report triggers a thesis re-check, not a sale.

---

*MU — Micron*
Breaks if: DRAM oversupply emerges and memory pricing collapses; hyperscalers cut AI capex meaningfully; Micron loses HBM market share to Samsung or SK Hynix.
Does NOT break if: stock drops 15–20% on broad market selloff; single quarter miss without guidance cut; general "AI bubble" noise without capex data changes.

*LLY — Eli Lilly*
Breaks if: orforglipron Phase 3 fails or shows serious safety issues; a competitor launches a clearly superior drug; regulatory action limits GLP-1 access or pricing; tirzepatide faces material biosimilar threat.
Does NOT break if: stock drops on broad biotech selloff; quarterly miss on competition narrative without volume data confirming; general "GLP-1 hype is over" sentiment.

*CEG — Constellation Energy*
Breaks if: AI capex slows materially and hyperscalers walk back power commitments; major nuclear safety incident shifts regulatory environment; Three Mile Island restart fails or is permanently delayed beyond 2028; federal nuclear policy reverses.
Does NOT break if: TMI restart slips by a year or two; quarterly miss on weather/operational items; sector rotation out of utilities on rate moves.

*LMT — Lockheed Martin*
Breaks if: F-35 program faces structural cancellation or major cost overruns forcing budget renegotiation; US defense budget growth reverses; sustained disarmament environment that actually crushes procurement; major fraud or program failure.
Does NOT break if: quarterly volatility on classified program write-downs; sector rotation; geopolitical de-escalation that doesn't reduce procurement.

*MA — Mastercard*
Breaks if: material regulatory action against interchange fees (US or EU); stablecoin/CBDC adoption disintermediates card networks at scale; consumer credit cycle turns severely and transaction volume drops; Visa wins materially more share in core payment volumes.
Does NOT break if: single quarter softness on consumer spending; stock drops on rate-cut delays; crypto/stablecoin headlines without actual volume migration.
